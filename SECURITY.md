# Security

Found something in momputer, or in one of the skills, that could hurt someone? Please tell me privately first.

**[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/Thisisily/momputer/security/advisories/new)** (GitHub keeps it between us until it's fixed.)

Please don't open a public issue for it. I'll answer within a few days, fix it, and credit you if you'd like.

The newest release is the one that gets fixes. The app tells you when a newer one is out.
