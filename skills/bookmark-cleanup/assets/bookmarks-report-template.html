How this was made, and what happens next

  1. Tick what you want and press Copy approval. Paste the line to your assistant.
  2. Your assistant writes a new file, bookmarks-tidy.html, with those changes applied, and bookmarks-removed.html with everything that was left out.
  3. In your browser, first save a backup with its own Export bookmarks. Then import bookmarks-tidy.html. The browser adds it next to what you have, usually in a folder called Imported.
  4. Look through the imported set. When you are happy with it, delete the old bookmarks yourself and drag the new ones into place. Your assistant never deletes or edits anything in the browser.
